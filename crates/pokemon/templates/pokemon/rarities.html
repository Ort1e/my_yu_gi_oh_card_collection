Recognising rarities and variants

A Pokémon card's rarity is printed on it: a small symbol at the bottom, next to the number (Japanese cards: letters, like SAR). Its variant (normal, holo, reverse holo) is not printed: you see it in the shine. Check, in this order:

Look atIf you seeIt's probably
The numberabove the set size: 067/064a chase card: illustration rare, special illustration rare, hyper rare, gold, rainbow…
a letter instead: R, G, Ban RGB Rare (the Mew cards of 30th Celebration)
letters before it: TG05, GG35, SV107a Trainer Gallery, Galarian Gallery or Shiny Vault card (Sword & Shield)

Full art: the picture covers the whole card, the text is printed over it. Full arts are the ultra rares, illustration rares, special illustration rares and gold cards: the app ticks it from the rarity, you can untick it.
